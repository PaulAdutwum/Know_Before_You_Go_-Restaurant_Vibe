## 🎨 Design Principles Applied

1. **Dark Theme** → Reduces eye strain, modern aesthetic
2. **High Contrast** → Easy readability (white on dark)
3. **Color Coding** → Green=good, Yellow=okay, Red=warning
4. **Card Design** → Organizes information cleanly
5. **Whitespace** → Breathing room, not cluttered
6. **Hierarchy** → Size/weight shows importance
7. **Consistency** → Same patterns throughout
8. **Responsive** → Works on all screen sizes
9. **Feedback** → Loading, errors, success states
10. **Delight** → Smooth animations, polish
